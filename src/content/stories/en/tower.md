# The Tower With No Stairs

## Darkness

The tower has swallowed its own stairs. Even the moon cannot find the door.

Write a bright message into the darkness. A room is much friendlier when it says hello.

## A pocket companion

Something small is purring inside your pocket. It has been waiting for you to learn its name.

Summon a cat. It knows this tower, but it refuses to go first.

## The missing stairs

The cat looks up at a door floating in the air. "I could jump," it says. "But you could repeat yourself."

Use a loop to draw six steps. The same little spell can build a whole staircase.

## The key that wandered

A key is sitting at the far side of the room. The cat considers this your problem.

Summon the key and send your companion to collect it. Tell the door when the cat arrives.

## The ghost corridor

The corridor is full of lonely ghosts. They do not need defeating. They need a party.

Invite three ghosts and let them drift. Give each one a moment in the spotlight.

## The silent machine

A brass machine has forgotten its song. Four empty bells hang above it.

Play C4, E4, G4, C5. You may discover a song it likes even better.

## A very solid wall

Someone has built a wall where the next doorway should be. The cat produces a tiny bundle from its bag.

Build the wall, then summon dynamite beside it. Stand back and try its EXPLODE spell.

## The star chamber

Beyond the dust is a room with a ceiling as big as the night. Every star is missing.

Scatter stars across the sky. Randomness makes a different universe every time.

## Your name in the sky

The tower has stairs again. The ghosts have a dance floor. The cat has decided to stay.

The final room asks who woke it. Tell it your name, write it into the sky, and give the tower a sound to remember.
